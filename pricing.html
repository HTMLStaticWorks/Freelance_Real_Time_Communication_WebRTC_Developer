Common Inquiries

Do you charge per minute for streaming?

No. I build the infrastructure and hand it over to you. You only pay the raw bandwidth and computing costs directly to your cloud provider (AWS, GCP, DigitalOcean).

Do you handle frontend UI development?

I focus heavily on the backend architecture, SFU logic, and the WebRTC signaling client-side SDKs. I typically integrate these SDKs into your existing React/Vue applications.

How do we start a project?

We begin with a discovery call to understand your requirements, followed by an architecture proposal and a fixed-price scope of work.
