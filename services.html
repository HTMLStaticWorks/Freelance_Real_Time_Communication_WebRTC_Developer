SFU Development & Scaling

We architect and deploy custom Selective Forwarding Units (SFUs) using Mediasoup or Janus. Ideal for applications requiring large multi-party video rooms without overloading client devices.

  • Horizontal scaling across worker nodes
  • Simulcast & Adaptive Bitrate (ABR)
  • Active speaker detection algorithms

TURN/STUN Deployment

Ensure your calls never fail due to strict corporate firewalls or symmetric NATs. We deploy globally distributed coTURN clusters to guarantee 99.9% connectivity.

  • Global edge routing
  • Secure TLS/DTLS encryption
  • Bandwidth limitation & auth mechanisms

Custom Signaling Servers

WebRTC doesn't dictate how you establish connections. I build extremely fast, bespoke WebSocket and gRPC signaling layers tailored strictly to your application's logic.

  • Highly concurrent Node.js / Rust backends
  • Redis Pub/Sub for multi-server scaling
  • Graceful error handling and reconnections
